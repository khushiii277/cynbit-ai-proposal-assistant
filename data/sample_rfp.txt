CLIENT RFP: AI-POWERED INTERNAL KNOWLEDGE AND PROPOSAL ASSISTANT

Background
We are a mid-sized professional services company. Employees currently search through PDFs, Word files, project summaries, and technical notes to answer internal questions and prepare initial client proposals.

Objectives
1. Allow users to upload PDF and TXT documents.
2. Search approved project and capability documents using natural-language queries.
3. Generate a first-draft technical proposal and statement of work (SOW) from a client brief.
4. Cite source documents used for recommendations.
5. Identify missing requirements, assumptions, risks, and questions for a discovery meeting.
6. Provide a simple browser-based interface.

Constraints
- Use a Python-based proof of concept.
- Keep human approval in the loop; do not automatically send proposals to clients.
- Do not use confidential documents in the demo.
- The prototype should be deployable on a small cloud instance.
- Use a modular design so a production data store and authentication can be added later.

Requested deliverables
- Requirements summary
- Proposed architecture and technology choices
- Phased implementation plan
- Team and effort estimate with assumptions
- Risks, dependencies, exclusions, and acceptance criteria

Please make any unknowns explicit rather than inventing project-specific facts or prices.
