======================================================================
REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL (RFP): CLOUD-NATIVE HEALTHCARE DASHBOARD
======================================================================

1. COMPANY BACKGROUND
HealthPulse Inc. is a leading provider of digital health monitoring solutions. 
We connect patients with specialist healthcare providers through remote health monitoring 
devices and telemetry tools.

2. PROJECT OVERVIEW
We are seeking an experienced software development partner to design, build, and deploy 
a secure, HIPAA-compliant Cloud-Native Patient Monitoring Dashboard. The platform will 
aggregate real-time data from IoT health devices (heart rate, blood pressure, oxygen levels), 
visualize patient trends, and send automated alerts to assigned clinicians when critical 
thresholds are breached.

3. KEY TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- Frontend: Web-based portal with real-time data streaming and interactive charts.
- Backend: High-performance microservices backend capable of handling 50,000+ continuous patient data streams.
- Security & Compliance: Full HIPAA compliance, end-to-end data encryption (at rest and in transit), and multi-factor role-based access control (RBAC).
- Notifications: Automated SMS/email/push alerts for medical emergency events based on configurable patient metrics.
- AI/Analytics Integration: Predictive anomaly detection module to flag potential health deteriorations before critical events occur.

4. DELIVERABLES EXPECTED FROM BIDDER
- Architectural Blueprint & System Design Document.
- Full Source Code Repository & CI/CD Pipeline.
- Comprehensive Testing Strategy (Unit, Integration, and Security Audits).
- Cloud Infrastructure Setup (AWS or Azure) with Infrastructure as Code (IaC).
- Post-launch Maintenance and Support Plan.

5. TIMELINE & BUDGET TARGETS
- Target MVP Launch: Within 16–20 weeks from contract execution.
- Estimated Budget Range: $120,000 – $180,000.

6. SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS
Please provide a comprehensive response detailing your recommended technical stack, team composition, milestone timeline, risk management strategy, and cost estimate.
